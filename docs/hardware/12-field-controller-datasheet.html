GFC-100

Gate Field Controller

Ethernet-connected gate & access controller with 4 relay outputs, 3 sensor inputs and RS-485

Features

  • ESP32-S3 dual-core 240 MHz MCU, 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE
  • Wired 10/100 Ethernet with hardware TCP/IP stack for deterministic, always-on connectivity
  • 4 SPDT relays for gate OPEN / CLOSE and two spare outputs, each with status LED
  • 3 protected contact inputs for open limit, closed limit and safety beam
  • Fused 12 V output to power the safety photo-beam
  • RS-485 port with fail-safe bias, termination jumper and surge protection
  • Robust 9–15 V input: resettable fuse, reverse-polarity and transient protection
  • USB-C service port: flash, log and debug with no adapter
  • Pluggable power, relay and sensor connectors: swap the controller without touching field wiring
  • Every terminal labelled on the board, all cable entries facing the board edge

Applications

  • Sliding and swing gate automation
  • Barrier arms and bollards
  • ALPR / LiDAR vehicle access systems
  • Networked dry-contact I/O nodes

Description

The GFC-100 is the gate-side controller of an automated vehicle access system. It receives commands from the access server over wired Ethernet and drives the gate motor controller through isolated dry relay contacts. It also watches the gate's limit switches and safety beam, so it can stop or reverse travel locally. A fused 12 V output powers the beam directly, and the RS-485 port connects loop detectors, displays and other field devices. All field connections are on pluggable or screw terminals at the board edges, with every pin labelled on the board.

The red, green and yellow status LEDs are driven by the firmware; the patterns shown are those of the standard gate firmware. The link LED follows the Ethernet PHY. Two more LEDs inside the RJ45 jack show link and activity. Each relay has its own LED next to it, lit while the coil is energised.

RS-485 termination: bridge solder jumper JP1 (silk "JP1 TERM") only on the two units at the ends of the bus.

Installation notes

  • Mount on four M3 standoffs (≥ 6 mm) in a weatherproof (IP65) enclosure; the mounting holes are tied to ground.
  • Keep metal parts and cable bundles clear of the antenna edge (top, middle 48 mm), including any metal back-plate.
  • Bring cables in through glands on the wall facing each connector: bottom for relays and sensors, right for Ethernet, RS-485 and beam, left for power.
  • Use a regulated 12 V DC supply rated ≥ 1 A plus the beam load.
  • Leave ≥ 30 mm in front of the pluggable headers (power, relays, sensors) for plugs and wire bend radius.
  • To replace a controller, unplug J1, J7 and J8 and unscrew J9 / J10: the field wiring stays in the plugs.
  • Connect relay outputs only to low-voltage control inputs of the gate motor controller.
