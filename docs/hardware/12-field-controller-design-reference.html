Board Specification Sheet · Hardware Revision 2.2

The Gate Field Controller is the gate-side node of the ALPR + LiDAR automated vehicle gate system. It drives the gate motor controller through dry relay contacts, reads the gate's limit switches and safety beam, reports to the server over wired Ethernet and offers RS-485 for field peripherals. This document specifies the board as designed in KiCad 10 (hardware/pcb/gate-field-controller/). The schematic diagrams in §10 are exported directly from that design.

1. Overview

Features

  • MCU: Espressif ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8: dual-core Xtensa LX7 up to 240 MHz, 16 MB flash, 8 MB octal PSRAM, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE, PCB antenna on a keep-out board edge
  • Ethernet: WIZnet W5500 (hardwired TCP/IP, 8 sockets, 32 KB buffer) on SPI. Auto-negotiating 10/100BASE-TX through a HanRun HR911105A magjack with link/activity LEDs
  • Gate drive: four 5 V SPDT relays (SRD-05VDC-SL-C) with NPN drivers, flyback diodes and per-channel "energised" LEDs, on one 12-way pluggable header (COM/NO/NC)
  • Field inputs: three volt-free contact inputs (open limit, closed limit, safety beam). Each has a series resistor, pull-up, RC filter and BAV99 rail clamps
  • Beam supply: fused 12 V output (500 mA PTC) for the safety-beam transmitter/receiver
  • RS-485: half-duplex SP3485 transceiver with fail-safe bias, solder-jumper 120 Ω termination and SM712 surge protection
  • Power: 12 V DC input with resettable fuse, reverse-polarity diode and 15 V TVS. 5 V / 3 A non-synchronous buck (TPS5430, SS34 catch diode) and 3.3 V / 1 A LDO
  • Service: USB-C (native USB Serial/JTAG) with ESD protection, BOOT/RESET buttons, UART0 and JTAG headers, four test points
  • Indicators: PWR, red/green/yellow status, Ethernet link, four relay-state LEDs

Applications

  • Sliding and swing-gate motor controllers with dry-contact OPEN / CLOSE command inputs
  • Barrier-arm and bollard controllers accepting pulse or maintained contacts
  • Any 12 V field node needing wired Ethernet, relay outputs and contact inputs in an IP65 box

3.2 Power from USB

USB VBUS feeds the +5V rail through D6 (SS14), so the logic runs from a laptop for flashing and bench tests without 12 V. D6 blocks back-feed from the on-board 5 V into the USB host. The four relay coils draw ≈ 290 mA together; with all relays energised the total can exceed a 500 mA USB 2.0 port. Use 12 V for relay tests.

InterfaceCharacteristics
Ethernet (J6)10BASE-T / 100BASE-TX, auto-negotiation (W5500 PMODE[2:0] = 111 via R24–R26). Integrated magnetics, with link and activity LEDs in the jack. SPI to the MCU (mode 0/3, up to 80 MHz per W5500). 33 Ω series termination on SCLK/MOSI. Interrupt on IO9, reset on IO8 with 10 kΩ pull-up

10. Schematic diagrams: sheet 1/7, top level

Hierarchical overview with the interface summary. The six sub-sheets follow.

Sheet 2/7: Power supply

J1 → F1 → D1 → +12V (D2 TVS, C1/C2) → U1 TPS5430 buck → +5V (L1, C4/C5, D4) → U2 AMS1117 → +3V3. PWR LED, test points, mounting holes.

Sheet 3/7: MCU, USB-C and programming

U3 ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 with decoupling, EN reset network (SW1), BOOT strap (SW2), USB-C with U4 ESD protection and series resistors, UART0 and JTAG headers.

Sheet 4/7: Ethernet (W5500 + RJ45)

U5 W5500, 25 MHz crystal network (Y1, C27/C28, R21, R22), AVDD ferrite filter, EXRES1 bias, PMODE straps, J6 HR911105A with LED drive.

Sheet 5/7: Relay outputs

Four identical channels: GPIO → 1 kΩ → SS8050 → relay coil (1N4148W flyback, energised LED) → COM/NO/NC on J7.

Sheet 6/7: Field inputs and beam power

J8 inputs, each through 330 Ω series, 10 kΩ pull-up, 100 nF and a BAV99 clamp to the GPIO. J9 beam supply through F2.

Sheet 7/7: RS-485 and status LEDs

U6 SP3485 with fail-safe bias, JP1 termination, D60 SM712 and J10. Status LEDs D61–D63, NET LED D64.
